## 2. What these analyses are, and what they are not

The technical reading of the cocoa contract listed on ICE Futures Europe (London) and the accompanying position indicator constitute **investment recommendations** within the meaning of Article 3(1)(35) of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014. They are produced and disseminated in accordance with Article 20 of that Regulation and with Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/958.

They are **general and strictly identical for all subscribers**. They constitute **neither a personal recommendation nor investment advice** within the meaning of Articles L541-1 *et seq.* of the French Monetary and Financial Code.

We do not tailor any analysis to a subscriber's situation, exposure or portfolio. We do not receive, transmit or execute any order. We do not manage any position on behalf of third parties. We are neither an investment services provider nor a financial investment adviser (*conseiller en investissements financiers*).

Analyses relating to the physical fundamentals of the market — agro-climatic conditions, arrivals, logistics, press review — are market information and do not relate to a financial instrument.

## 3. What the indicators mean

| Indicator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OPEN** | The technical reading is oriented upwards: the signals considered converge towards prices holding or firming over the stated horizon. |
| **MONITOR** | The reading is not clear-cut: the signals diverge or offset one another, and no definite direction emerges over the stated horizon. |
| **HEDGE** | The technical reading is oriented downwards: the signals considered converge towards prices weakening over the stated horizon. |

**Horizon**: D+1. The indicator is established at the close of a reference session and relates to the following trading session. It says nothing beyond that.

None of these three indicators is an instruction to trade. They express a market reading, general and identical for all subscribers; the operation to be drawn from it — selling, buying, storing, hedging or doing nothing — is for the subscriber alone, in the light of their own exposure.

**Confidence score**: rated from 1 to 5, it expresses the degree of convergence of the signals considered. A low score indicates a weakly determined reading.

**Regime**: characterises the trend observed over the recent period. Possible values: bullish, bearish, transitional.

## 4. Methodology — summary

The position indicator results from the aggregation, by a proprietary model, of two families of inputs:

- **Market data**: price history, volumes and open interest of the ICE Futures Europe cocoa contract, from which standard technical indicators are derived (moving average convergence divergence, relative strength, stochastic, average true range, volume-to-open-interest ratio).
- **Fundamental data**: agro-climatic conditions in origin countries, arrivals, port logistics, institutional publications and the specialist press.

These inputs are normalised and then combined according to a **proprietary scoring formula**, whose parameters and weightings constitute a trade secret and are not published. The result is mapped onto the OPEN / MONITOR / HEDGE scale and is subject to **daily human review**, under the responsibility of the publication director.

**Known limitations.** The model is calibrated on observed market regimes; a regime change, an exogenous shock — a public decision by a producing country, a major climatic event, a regulatory development — or degraded liquidity may invalidate it. It takes no account of any data specific to a subscriber.

**Facts and opinions.** In each edition, market data and sourced facts are distinguished from interpretations, projections and price targets, which are identified as such. Material sources are indicated; those considered unreliable or unverifiable are flagged as such.

**Timestamping.** Each edition states the time at which production was completed, the time of dissemination, and the time of the prices to which it refers.

## 6. Interests and conflicts of interest
