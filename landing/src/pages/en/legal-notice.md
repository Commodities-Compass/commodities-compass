## 5. Nature of the service

COMMODITIES COMPASS SAS publishes and operates **Compass CC**, a subscription publication providing daily analysis of the cocoa market, delivered online in text and audio form.

Each edition comprises a reading of physical fundamentals — agro-climatic conditions in origin countries, arrivals, port logistics, press review — and a technical reading of the cocoa contract listed on ICE Futures Europe (London), together with a position indicator (OPEN, MONITOR, HEDGE) produced by a proprietary algorithm.

**Classification.** The technical reading and the position indicator constitute **investment recommendations** within the meaning of Article 3(1)(35) of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014, and are produced and disseminated in accordance with Article 20 of that Regulation and with Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/958. Production arrangements, methodology, the distribution of recommendations and the statement of interests are set out on the [Methodology and Transparency](https://www.com-compass.com/methodologie) page.

**What the service is not.** The analyses are **general and identical for all subscribers**. They constitute neither a personal recommendation nor investment advice within the meaning of Articles L541-1 *et seq.* of the French Monetary and Financial Code. COMMODITIES COMPASS SAS does not tailor any analysis to a subscriber's situation, exposure or portfolio; does not receive, transmit or execute any order; and does not manage any position on behalf of third parties. It is neither an investment services provider nor a financial investment adviser (*conseiller en investissements financiers*).

Each subscriber remains solely responsible for their own decisions, whether commercial or financial, and bears their consequences alone.
