# Pricing and Terms

## What we sell

**Compass CC** — a subscription publication providing daily analysis of the cocoa market, delivered online in text and audio form.

Each edition comprises a reading of physical fundamentals — agro-climatic conditions in origin countries, arrivals, port logistics, press review — and a technical reading of the cocoa contract listed on ICE Futures Europe (London), together with a position indicator produced by our algorithm.

The service is intended for professionals in the cocoa supply chain — exporters, cooperatives, traders, processors and institutions — as well as for individuals subscribing in their own name.

**The technical reading constitutes an investment recommendation within the meaning of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014.** It is general and identical for all subscribers: it constitutes neither a personal recommendation nor investment advice. See the [Methodology and Transparency](https://www.com-compass.com/methodologie) page. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

## How it is billed

- **Recurring subscription**, billed **monthly**, in advance.
- **In euros (EUR)**, excluding taxes.
- Automatic charge to the payment method registered in the customer area, at each monthly instalment, until termination.
- **Commitment period: 12 months**, renewed by tacit agreement for successive 12-month periods.

## How the price is set

There is no public price list. **Pricing is set by quotation**, according to the scope agreed: modules activated, markets and products covered, number of users, volume and level of support. Seven offers are available, each corresponding to a distinct scope.

The quotation states the exact monthly amount, the date of the first instalment and the commitment period. No amount is charged before acceptance of the quotation and subscription.

To obtain a quotation: contact@com-compass.com.

## Taxes

Prices are stated exclusive of tax. **Any tax due in the customer's country — in particular local value added tax — is added to the price and borne by the customer.** Services supplied to a customer established outside the European Union are not subject to French value added tax.

## Price revision

Prices may be revised at each renewal, subject to three months' notice, capped at the higher of the annual variation in the **Syntec index** or **3%**. Beyond that, the customer may decline the revision and terminate the subscription at the end of the current period, without penalty.

## How to terminate

- **From the customer area**, at any time, in a few clicks; or in writing to contact@com-compass.com.
- Termination must take place **no later than 30 days before the end** of the current period.
- It takes effect **at the end of the current commitment period**. The service remains accessible until that date, and the monthly instalments falling due until then remain payable.
- **No pro rata refund** is made for the period already billed, except where termination is due to Compass's breach or is initiated by Compass.

**Non-professional customers**: a 14-day right of withdrawal applies on the conditions set out in Article 21 of the Terms and Conditions of Sale; prior notice is sent before each renewal; the online termination function is permanently accessible from the customer area.
